Slotting services and slotting machining

Slotting is a machining process in which a broach or slotting tool performs reciprocating motion to remove material from the workpiece surface. Metal slotting is primarily used for hard-to-reach surfaces such as internal cavities, keyways, and splines.

This method enables machining of parts to a high degree of accuracy. Our company performs slotting on modern equipment that ensures reliability and high quality of the finished product. We work with various metals including steel, cast iron, and non-ferrous alloys.

Gear slotting services and gear slotting machining

Gear slotting is a specialized form of mechanical machining used to produce gears, pinion shafts, and other components with a tooth profile. Gear slotting is performed on dedicated machines using high-precision tooling.

This method is especially effective for cutting teeth on parts with internal gearing, as well as for manufacturing gear clusters where milling is limited by the design of the component. Our production facility guarantees compliance with all geometric parameters and tolerances per customer drawings.

We ensure high positioning accuracy and smooth operation of finished mechanisms through strict quality control at every stage of slotting.
