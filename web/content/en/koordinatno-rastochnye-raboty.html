What is boring work?

Boring operations are a type of mechanical machining using a group of metal-cutting machines designed for processing large workpieces in serial and custom production.

Boring operations include: cylindrical turning, boring, counterboring, cylindrical and face milling, drilling, facing, and external and internal threading.
