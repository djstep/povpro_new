Asbestos-free friction components

Among the wide range of parts we manufacture, asbestos-free friction components for various braking systems hold a special place. They perform under high loads and multi-shift operation, across a broad temperature range, at a relatively low cost.

These characteristics were achieved through a unique asbestos-free friction material developed by our specialists, which meets stringent GOST requirements, Russian sanitary and epidemiological standards, and UNECE regulations.
