Technological Features

TST series die-casting machines feature a uniformly accelerated first phase that prevents the "reflected wave" effect. As a result, castings achieve higher density and mixing of metal and air in the injection chamber is prevented.

Metal blow-through is also eliminated when operating at maximum mold clamping force, thanks to a "controlled surge" during deceleration at the end of mold filling.

Thanks to these unique features of the production systems, you can obtain castings of any complexity — from thin-walled (from 0.5 mm) to thick-walled — meeting strict requirements for porosity and geometric accuracy.

Toggle system bushings, tie bars, and pins are manufactured in Italy using proprietary technologies. Their distinguishing characteristics are high wear resistance and reliability. In-house research and continuous monitoring of global die-casting technology trends have enabled the development of equipment whose capabilities closely match the requirements of foundry professionals.
